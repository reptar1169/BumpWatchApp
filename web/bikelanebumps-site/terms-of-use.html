1. What BumpWatch Is

BumpWatch is a community-sourced project: a Watch app that logs bike-lane bumps as you ride, and a website that maps everything anyone has submitted. It's built and run by a single developer, not a company, and there's no account system on either side.

2. Using the Watch App

BumpWatch needs Location, Motion, and Health permissions to record a ride — you'll be asked to approve these the first time you use it, and you can withdraw them at any time in your Watch or iPhone's Settings, which will stop the app from being able to record. By recording and uploading a ride, you agree that its data — including exact GPS locations — becomes public on this website, as described in the Privacy Policy.

BumpWatch is a data-logging tool, not a safety device. It doesn't warn you about hazards in real time, and using it is never a substitute for riding attentively and using your own judgment about road conditions, traffic, and your surroundings.

4. The Data on This Site

The heatmap and stats on bikelanebumps.org are built entirely from rider-submitted data. We don't independently verify the accuracy, completeness, or currency of any bump or ride shown on the map, and road conditions can change at any time after data is recorded. Don't treat the map as an official or authoritative source on road conditions — for that, contact your local public works department.
